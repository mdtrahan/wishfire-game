# Wishfire Migration Atlas - Editorial Field Manual

## Format

- Landscape A4: 841.89 x 595.28 pt
- Margins: 38 pt left/right, 34 pt top, 28 pt bottom
- Grid: 12 columns, 14 pt gutters, 8 pt baseline
- Page types: opener, evidence spread, diagram spread, matrix, and handoff appendix

## Typography

- Display: Georgia Bold, 27-34 pt, tight leading
- Section label: Arial Bold, 7.5 pt, tracked uppercase
- Body: Arial, 8.4-9.4 pt, 1.28-1.38 leading
- Data labels: Arial Bold, 7-8 pt
- Notes and sources: Arial, 6.8-7.4 pt
- Maximum paragraph measure: 62 characters when possible

## Color roles

- Paper: `#F7F2E8`
- Paper bright: `#FCFAF5`
- Ink: `#17232D`
- Secondary ink: `#58646D`
- Rule: `#CBD1D0`
- Cyan: `#00A8B5`
- Violet: `#7357B3`
- Orange: `#DE7A3E`
- Green: `#2E8B68`
- Rose: `#C45262`
- Gold: `#B98522`

Color always supports a written label, line style, number, or position. The document remains understandable in grayscale.

## Page grammar

- The running head names the study and the current chapter.
- The folio sits on the outer bottom edge.
- Section openers use one large serif statement and one system diagram.
- Evidence spreads pair one dominant figure with numbered observations.
- Matrices use thin rules, square cells, and quiet tints instead of rounded cards.
- Captions identify the figure, source, and purpose.

## Accessibility

- Body text stays selectable and searchable.
- Draw order follows reading order: heading, premise, figure, figure note, evidence, footer.
- Figure titles and notes carry the information needed to interpret diagrams.
- Fonts are embedded where the PDF producer supports embedding.
- Contrast is checked against the warm paper field.
