# Rust app DOX (wf_app)

## Purpose
- The browser game as one Rust binary: macroquad client (ported from the slice `wf_client` at 9c142b5f) over `simulation_core::combat_abi::World`, the typed combat host.

## Contracts
- The sim is authoritative. `game.rs` owns one `wf_host::Session` (30 Hz pacing, stage director, rewards, save) and the client reads `Snapshot` + typed `Event`s; it never writes sim state. Skill Spotlight (ORKA-63bf.21): `Scene::new` sets `session.spotlight = SpotlightConfig::DEFAULT`, so the sim schedules spotlights for offensive specials (its own gates: a boss target or three covered enemies, 12 s between spotlights, 30 s per hero) and freezes the combat clock around them; `render/spotlight.rs` presents charge, the 80% cover and the foreground layers from `Snapshot::spotlight`, and the frame loop calls `session.release_spotlight()` when `Spotlight::ready` says the featured effects have settled, which ends the Tail phase and resumes combat. Dev key S cycles the presets through `session.set_spotlight`.
- `platform.rs` is the browser seam: `wf_storage_*` (localStorage), `wf_session_*` (sessionStorage), `wf_random_u32` (crypto) imports from the miniquad plugin in `web/index.html` (files under `.wf-save/` natively), `now_ms` (pacing) and `epoch_ms` (`Date.now` stamps in saves). Save keys `orka.hero_progress.v2` and `wishfire.equipment-economy.v5`, shared with the JS game.
- `game.rs` also owns the quest system (`wf_host::progression::CombatProgression`): booted after the economy sync; each tick observes milestones before the host step, then records committed enemy defeats, drained `wave_clear_events` and queued gear collections; `claim_objective`/`train`/`spend_level_up`/`star_up` are the UI entry points. Quest save key `orka.combat_progression.v1` (numbers may serialize as `0.0` where JS wrote `0`; both load).
- `game.rs` also owns the Bag economy (`wf_host::equipment::EquipmentStorage`): synced at boot and whenever Bag receipts or gold change; every tick feeds the 15 s Bag clock. Equipment arrives as battlefield orbs (ORKA-63bf.20/.21, owner specs 2026-11-04; the conveyor strip is gone and the arena band takes its 92 px: `camera::BAND_BOTTOM` 522, hero clocks at 533.6, quest tracker `chrome::QUEST_TOP`). `ui/orbs.rs`: an enemy `Died` event drops an orb with `ORB_DROP_CHANCE` (10%; the item is rolled with the current Bag level's rarity weights and saved by `drop_orbs`). When `Session::subwave_completions` advances, the orbs on the field become eligible; an eligible orb is picked up when a living hero's feet are within the pickup circle (diameter 3 hero body heights, in world units), in one `collect_orbs(ids)` transaction that judges each item (see the ORKA-63bf.34 bullet below), and exactly the settled orbs then fly to the nearest such hero. Orbs out of reach, and upgrades blocked by a full Bag, stay where they fell; nothing is collected while a Bag window is open. The field holds `wf_meta::lamp::ORB_LIMIT` (40) orbs so an idle or full-Bag party cannot pile them up (ORKA-63bf.22: 2,488 orbs made a 564 KB save that every action rewrote): a drop on a full field displaces the oldest orb of the lowest rarity, which fades out; an older oversized save is trimmed by the same rule when it loads. The orb field owns only positions and motion; saved orbs it has no position for (a reload, old conveyor leftovers) are scattered around the party and are eligible. `ui/lamp.rs` is the bag icon beside the quest tracker (half its old size, same x centre, white `stored/capacity`, the count waits for orbs in flight), the Bag progress bar that slides out of it, and the equipment windows as page elements. The bar's presentation is the **Cascade Fill** ceremony (the owner's name for this pattern; future progress bars follow it): it comes out only when the Bag has been cleared (`BagBar::request`: a completed dismantle, or the bag tapped or its window closed with nothing stored and Essence held; ORKA-63bf.34), rests a 0.5 s beat, always comes out empty and fills left to right up to the saved progress (owner 2026-11-05, ORKA-63bf.32: a bar that came out part-filled read as a right-to-left fill; the fill never goes backwards except the reset after a full bar) (1.2 s for a whole bar, never under 0.6 s), applies one `upgrade` at the end of each full bar with a white flash and a bag pop, rests another beat and continues with the overflow, then holds 2.5 s after the last fill before sliding back. The bar carries no numbers and fills in the boss timer's blue (`chrome::TIMER_BLUE`). While it plays, input is locked (`LampUi::locks_input`: taps, page clicks and keys are dropped and a transparent `#wf-input-lock` covers the frame); combat keeps running, and the lock holds only on frames where the ceremony advanced, so a paused clock or a covered bag icon cannot leave the game locked. Essence from other sources and combat-time points are included the next time it comes out; levels are never applied unseen. The Bag Upgrade odds window is still built but has no entry point (open owner decision).
- `ui/` splits by surface. Canvas (unchanged): the in-fight HUD in the JS 360x640 layout: arena band 42..430 (`camera::BAND_*` = COMBAT_VIEW), progress HUD and Summon Boss, boss HUD + stats popover, party buff row and eight hero clocks with buff badges, the equipment orbs on the battlefield, the bag icon with its sliding Bag progress bar, the COT resources row and the quest objective box, plus the opening narrative scene. Canvas text is Inter Regular (`ui/text.rs`).
- Page elements are placed in CSS px with `Portrait::page_frame` / `page_size`, never `Portrait::frame` or `screen_width()`: miniquad keeps the pixel ratio it saw at load, so its logical px stop being CSS px once the ratio changes (ORKA-63bf.31). The loader's `watchDpr` keeps the canvas backing at the live ratio each frame, because emulated ratio changes (preview panes) raise no event.
- Menus and overlays are page elements driven from Rust (ORKA-63bf.19). `ui/dom.rs` is the helper: each frame `Ui::web` describes the `#wf-ui` stage (placed at the 360x640 frame, `--s` = CSS px per logical px) as a `Node` tree and `Dom::render` diffs it into real elements through the generic `wf_dom_*` element bridge in `web/index.html` (create, text, attr, unattr, insert, remove, listen). The bridge is the only JS and knows nothing about the game; a click reports its element handle and timestamp to `wf_dom_event`, queued for `Ui::web_clicks` (innermost listening element per click; `Act::Stay` on a panel keeps the click from its backdrop). Keys force a fresh element instead of morphing one screen into another.
- Element trees, ids and class names are main's (ea8bc3f2), and `ui/css/*.css` are main's rules copied verbatim (first line names the source): `hero.css`, `nav.css`, `modal_close.css`, `progression.css` (Growth dialog), `quest.css` (wallet, ladder, skip / paused-battle dialogs), `lamp.css` + `bag_upgrade.css` (cache, review, dismantle, Bag upgrade), `receipt.css`. `stage.css` and `lamp_adapter.css` adapt main's `position:fixed` overlays to the stage; `lamp_adapter.css` also carries one owner change (ORKA-63bf.23): the dismantle window uses the cache's five-column grid and full tile (level, CP) instead of main's smaller art-only tiles, so equipment looks the same in the cache, review and dismantle windows; `quest_map.css` and `vault.css` restate screens main draws on its canvas. Art uses main's paths under `assets/` (staged by the build script, versioned with `WF_ART_REV`); SVG icons are `<img>` data URIs because the bridge creates HTML elements only.
- Menu behaviour follows main: POWER UP opens the Growth dialog over the current screen (combat pauses, no departure); a HUD party icon opens its Party Skills entry; a hero clock opens that hero's Special on the Hero screen; the entry fade is a page element above the menus. Where main measures in JS, Rust computes the same numbers: Growth stage height (`layoutClose`), gear-cell centring (`assets::gear_cells`), fanfare particle offsets. Not reproduced: focus management, scroll-into-view of a focused Party Skill, glyph-cropped close marks and stat numbers (text with a small CSS nudge instead).
- Menu parity is checked against main with `output/fable-reference/tools/compare.mjs` (Hero) and `compare-screens.mjs` (other screens) in the main checkout's output folder.
- Developer controls are main's two pills at the frame's top right on every screen (`ui/dev.rs`, `css/dev.css`; the slice's canvas menu is gone), scaled by main's control scale `--cs` = min(1, `--s`): DEV opens Dev Tools, DEV2 toggles the diagnostics overlay (`debug.rs`). Dev Tools (ORKA-63bf.25, this build's own design) pauses combat while open and has one section per thing a developer tunes, all buttons because the element bridge reports clicks only: Run (pause, single step, 0.25x to 4x which is the host's range, restart, Skill Spotlight preset), Stage (chapter and wave steps, first / last wave, boss; `Game::dev_stage` rewrites the saved stage checkpoint), Party (roster members in or out for this page session via `game::toggle_member`, level steps and presets via `HeroProgressStore::set_level`), Economy (gold, Essence, Incense grants; drop 10 orbs, clear the field, a session drop rate, Play Cascade Fill), View (diagnostics, hero pickup circles) and Saves (Reset game with a recovery snapshot under `wishfire.qa.reset-backup.v1`, Restore snapshot). Level, stage, party and save changes rewrite the saves and the frame loop rebuilds the session from them. The panel reads the live game each frame (`DevState`): roster size, wave count and enemy names come from the game, so new heroes, enemies or wave counts appear without panel changes. `Ui::reset` keeps the page layer when the menu state is rebuilt; replacing the `Dom` orphans the old elements on the page (the cause of the "Close does nothing" bug after RESET GAME).
- Restart run (Dev Tools, key R) starts from zero (owner 2026-11-05, ORKA-63bf.28): it clears every tracked save like RESET GAME (`game::reset_saves`, recovery snapshot kept) so Bag level, items, chapter progress and resources are all back at the start, then rebuilds the session on the next seed and stays on the combat screen. Cascade Fill settles each full bar with `complete_fill`, never `upgrade`: no Bag level is gained until hero EXP exists (ORKA-63bf.26). Its timing: hold 0.4 s (0.6 s before ORKA-63bf.32) after the last fill; when the slide-out completes the bar's far end throws 14 small round sparks. The bar's bag-side end lies under the bag art (`BAR_TUCK`), so its rounded cap never shows. When the last fill ends the leftover Essence (less than one bar) moves into the bar's saved progress points (`EquipmentStorage::bank_essence`), so the Essence counter reads zero after every cascade; the bar is 9 px high. Its length out of the bag is 55.2 px (`BAR_REACH`, 40% shorter than the first 92; ORKA-63bf.36), with the bag-side end unmoved. A dismantle screen asked for while a CP float is rising (Auto Equip, Done / Dismantle) opens when the last float has finished (`LampUi::disposal_waiting`).
- Boss battles (ORKA-63bf.28): `chrome::draw` hides the chapter progress while `CombatModel::boss` is set and draws the boss block (name, inspect, HP and timer bars) at `BOSS_HUD_SCALE` 0.8 from `HUD_TOP`; the progress returns with ordinary waves.
- Wave chest presentation (ORKA-63bf.28): the WFM host's chest sends no `ChestDropped` / `ChestOpened` events, so `Rewards::observe` reads the landing bounce and the opening sparkle (`chest-sparkle`) off consecutive snapshots.
- Resource drops (ORKA-63bf.33, owner 2026-11-05; temporary until a hero EXP system exists): one roll per kill (`wf_meta::lamp::field_drop`) leaves an equipment orb (10%), 25 Gold (5%), 3 Essence (4%), one hero ticket for a random hero that can still level (3%), or nothing. `OrbField` keeps them as `Loot` pickups, drawn with the HUD's own icons (`chrome::resource_icon`, `ticket_art`); they follow the orb pickup rules (completed subwave, pickup circle, wave-chest sweep), need no Bag slot and are not saved while on the field (a reload loses unpicked ones; at most `LOOT_LIMIT` lie there). `OrbField::collected` hands each to `Game::award_loot` when its flight starts. The HUD's Gold pill shows the party's real Gold (main drew a fixed 999,999,999). Dev Tools: Orbs row, Drop loot.
- Stranded orbs (ORKA-63bf.27, owner 2026-11-05): while the wave chest is on a cleared field (`Snapshot::chests` not empty, no living enemy) `OrbField::update` ignores the pickup circle and sends every orb on the field to its nearest living hero; the Bag's free slots still decide what is stored, so orbs that do not fit stay.
- Boot lands on the Quests map; `?qa&seed=N` boots straight into combat (`startCombatForQA`) and fixes the seed; keys 1/2/4 speed, P pause, N step, R restart, D diagnostics, S spotlight preset, B summons an available boss (same as the Summon Boss button). `wasm_exports.wf_app_probe(i)`: 0 step, 1 wave, 2 combat time, 3 hash low bits, 4 live enemies, 5 fps, 6 boss state (0 locked, 1 available, 2 armed, 3 active), 7 boss seconds left, 8 cleared bosses.
- Art comes from `gen/` (packed by `rust/wf_pack` from `web-runner/assets/puppets` + chapter arenas) and `web-runner/assets/rust-combat/manifest.json` (effects, decals, font). `build.rs` fingerprints both; a stale atlas fails loading with a visible message.
- Build and stage with `tools/build_wf_app.sh`; review at `tools/serve_wf_app.sh` on 127.0.0.1:8807. `gen/`, `web/gen`, `web/art`, `web/*.wasm` stay untracked.
- Tests: `cargo test -p wf_app` (needs `gen/` packed). Client scenarios run on the real host loop through `testing::HostRun` (a seeded `wf_host::Session`, level-1 party, one 30 Hz step per `step()`, last snapshot kept across attempt gaps): camera containment through the Chapter 1 boss and attack facing over 18000 steps. Two `#[ignore]`d tests are bounded crowd search tools, not gates. Retired slice-only scenarios (sim spotlight playback, Healing Spring glow, Mirewing art, the 32-High-Orc crowd pin, the boss-intro camera cut) are noted in place with their reasons (ORKA-63bf.12).
- No equipment choices (ORKA-63bf.34, owner 2026-11-05): orbs are judged at pickup by the save (`collect_orbs` returns `OrbPickup`): an item that raises some hero's CP is stored, anything else is dismantled at once and its Essence is held; both fly to the hero, and only stored flights count for the bag number (`OrbField::flying`). An upgrade a full Bag turned away is marked `blocked` and asked about again only when a slot is free, so a full Bag costs no transaction per frame. `LampUi::open_bag`: a tap on the bag runs Auto-Equip at once and shows the cache window with the heroes, the CP floats and the just-equipped items (`equipped`); when the floats end it shows the dismantle screen for items that stopped being upgrades (Dismantle all), or closes if none are left. `LampUi::settle` plays the Cascade Fill when the Bag holds nothing and Essence is held: after the window closes, or on a bag tap with an empty Bag (no window). Main's review window, hero choice, Equip, Auto-Equip, Done / Dismantle and Send to disposal are not built; tiles are not pressable. Browser check: scratch `bag34.mjs` (bare party stores all; equipped party dismantles at pickup; empty-Bag tap plays the bar).
- Battle re-entry (ORKA-63bf.35, owner 2026-11-05): with a battle waiting, the Quests nav button pressed on another screen (Hero, Vault) goes straight back into combat (`StoryFlow::navigate` returns `ResumeCombat`), like the Vault's back-to-combat button. The Battle paused choice (Quit Battle / Continue Battle) is skipped there, not removed: it still opens when Quests is pressed on the combat screen itself, which is the only way to quit a battle. First entry (map, START) is unchanged.
- CP floats (ORKA-63bf.38, owner 2026-11-05): each `.lamp-cp-feedback` is a child of its hero's portrait button in the cache window (`left:50%`, vertically centred), not an overlay element placed by a guessed row height, so it starts on that hero at any window size and rises from there.
