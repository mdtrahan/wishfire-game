## Progress and transactions

Active combat time and Essence earned through existing gameplay fill one smooth progress bar toward the entire next-level cost. Every 15 seconds on the active combat clock grants 1 Bag progress point; paused, background and menu time grant none. Ordinary, pack and lone kills grant no points. Boss deaths grant 5 points as a participation reward. Points add no Essence, never convert to Essence and cannot fund other purchases. The equipment economy retains the save field `lamp.killPoints` for Bag progress points; missing or corrupt values load as zero. Tick receipts use `tick:<battleId>:<n>` with a 1-based completed tick index; boss receipts keep the enemy UID domain. The HeroProgress outbox saves each award before the equipment save records points and receipt ranges together, then acknowledges it. Both domain types share the newest-64-domain insertion-order window. Reload replays pending awards safely and recovers the next tick index from pending or settled receipts. Partial seconds toward the next tick may reset on reload; continuous battle changes preserve them. MAX emits no ticks and awards no points. Level Up spends Bag progress points first, then Essence for the remaining cost, carries surplus points forward, and immediately grants one earned Bag level after the durable save succeeds. Reaching the target, opening a menu, syncing and clock expiry never grant levels automatically. The bar caps visually at 100%; surplus Essence remains available after purchase. No intermediate notches, installment purchases, construction deadline or speed-up control remain. Existing Essence income remains unchanged.

Preserve current tuning: 100 Bag levels; capacity `20 + round(10 × (level − 1)^1.5)`; next-level cost for `n = level − 1`:

`max(round25((100 + 200*n - 10*n^2 + 5.35*n^3) * (1 + 0.04*sin(2*pi*n/10))), round25-up(5 * capacity(n)))`

`round25(x) = Math.round(x/25)*25`; `round25-up(x) = Math.ceil(x/25)*25`. This cubic trend adds a gentle ±4% wave and rounds costs to 25. The capacity floor is minimum 5x capacity per level; owner guidance 5-7x. It never raises the current reference costs. High Essence supply from dismantling surplus Bag items motivates the steeper curve. Keep existing fractional rarity weights, draw cadence, item rolls and Hero EXP. Capacity and odds apply to future generation without rerolling stored equipment. MAX cannot spend again.

Cost stays flat within each level and rises across all 99 payable levels. Every cost is a safe integer and a multiple of 25; minimum step 200. References: L1 100, L2 300, L3 525, L4 775, L5 1100, L6 1525, L7 2050, L8 2725, L10 4875, L11 6450, L15 16000, L20 36125, L21 42900, L31 141550, L51 653850, L71 1800150, L91 3837250, L99 4770375. The first 20 costs total 205900; tier 99 (level 100) has null cost. Existing saves keep their earned level and balances without refund; the next upgrade uses the new cost.

Tuning constants are owner-adjustable at any time without re-approval.

Transactions retain the Web Lock and expected-tier guard. Save failures or stale/concurrent requests cannot spend twice, promote twice or display an unsaved reward. Supported v5 paid-stage saves receive `floor(levelCost × paidStage / 4)` Essence credit once, including fully paid construction. Keep their earned level and other balances intact. Remove imported stage/deadline fields from the next saved record; preserve the original raw record at `wishfire.equipment-economy.v5.before-bag-upgrades`.
