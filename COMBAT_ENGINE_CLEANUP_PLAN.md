# Real-time combat engine cleanup

Status: implemented in the ORKA-p11 worktree and ready for owner human QA. All four approved five-active kits, seconds-based scheduling and effects, displacement, rewards, Rust outcome recovery, and elemental impact assets are present. Automated validation passed 887 tests with 3 skips, the 42-test hot-file gate, 29 Rust tests, and an offline release check with all 111 VFX frames. Live QA confirmed overlapping waves, boss-gate activation and boss spawn, frozen pause state, and defeat recovery. Changes remain uncommitted. The player living guide states the active combat contract; the older hero-command specification in `COMBAT_MIGRATION_PLAN.md` is historical where it conflicts with this plan.

## Contract to preserve

- Four independently statted heroes are the attack sources. There are no hidden Pal attacks or Pal-speed assignments.
- Each hero has five active skills alongside a separate basic attack. Hero levels and the other existing progression layers carry the power chase; these five skills have no individual unlocks or ranks. Skill cooldowns determine when skills act. Basic attacks have independent hero clocks and a small deterministic stagger, so two basics never start in the same frame. Other actors keep acting while one actor casts. Each hero starts one action at a time; different heroes may act together.
- Combat has no playable turns, action slots, SP, personal FLOW, or AF orb awards. Effects and cooldowns use seconds.
- Every attack finds a living target within range when it starts. Ordinary hero attacks share the encounter-order focus target while it remains eligible; area skills follow their own target rules. At impact, validate the launched hit's target identity, life state, and range. A target beyond the attack line takes no hit; any retarget requires a fresh in-range selection. Area and zone effects also exclude enemies beyond that line.
- Enemies enter from off-stage, approach their stopping points, then attack repeatedly on individual clocks until they or the heroes die. Enemy travel time does not alter attack interval. Boss patterns can override the normal melee/ranged rates.
- After Provoke and Cover, enemies choose the first living, in-range hero in stable encounter order. With no eligible hero, an enemy attack remains ready.
- Pause and background state freeze the combat simulation clock, cooldowns, effects, movement and pending impacts. Resume from the frozen state without catch-up attacks or elapsed-time damage.
- Keep the current stage loop: four waves per boss, ten stages per region, a roughly three-second walking interval, a boss button that appears when armed, and repeatable wave farming. Heroes keep their HP across ordinary waves. A boss clear fully restores the party, revives KO heroes and clears debuffs before the next stage. Defeat restores the party and returns it to wave 1 of the current stage. Death and retries cost no resource.
- Preserve the established bidirectional damage calculation, resistance/crit/final-damage order, equipment progression, EXP, ordinary-kill Gold accounting, and visual attack-to-impact alignment unless a focused test proves they require repair. Ordinary kills may gain fragment drops under the separate loot design; boss-kill milestones are not a resource gate.

## Cutover sequence

Before the first runtime edit, record a recoverable checkpoint of this worktree's current uncommitted diff and a focused test baseline. Keep each phase reviewable with its own focused proof. Do not commit or merge until the owner gives human QA approval. The action arbitration in step 2 is an implementation proposal; it must be made visible in cooldown behavior and verified in play.

1. **Lock the five kits and baseline.** Record the five active skill IDs, simultaneous activation rule and seconds-based cooldown/effect durations for each hero. Measure current four-hero damage, enemy time to kill, wave transition, boss retry and save/reload in deterministic fixtures. 2. **Make one real-time action clock.** Reuse `realtimeCooldown.mjs` for interval math. Store each actor's next basic time and each active skill's next-ready time in encounter state. Replace its one-ready-actor limit and the overdue-basic skip in `TickRealtimeHeroBasics`. Give each actor one cast occupancy; projectile flight continues after occupancy ends and never blocks other actors. Service an overdue basic before another ready skill from that actor after its current cast, then resume stable skill priority. Bound long-frame catch-up to one start per actor per tick, preserve overdue due times, and spread colliding basic starts by one frame without changing their long-run interval. Cooldown starts when the skill casts. Reset clocks only on a documented encounter/death transition.
3. **Move enemy actions to the same tick.** An engaged enemy checks range, applies existing Provoke/Cover priority and the approved fallback target rule, then starts its attack when its own interval is due. 6. **Delete retired state and UI.** Inventory Split, Chain Strike, Arcane Pulse, double attacks and other basic-hit hooks against the approved five-skill kits before retaining any extra hit. ## Acceptance and proof

- A deterministic two-wave fixture records actor UID, action/skill ID, due time, cast time, impact time, target UID and HP. Each hero and enemy acts on its own clock; only colliding basic starts are separated by a frame. Sustained ready skills cannot starve basics, and long frames use bounded catch-up. No old turn owner is invoked.
- All four heroes can basic attack and cast each selected active skill repeatedly on cooldown. A due basic is delayed only by that hero's actual cast, then fires. No SP/FLOW field or charge event affects readiness.
- Melee and ranged enemies attack repeatedly after engaging. A later entrant is not stranded idle; travel speed and attack rate remain independent. Boss-specific timing still wins over the baseline band.
- Basic/projectile impact and HP loss occur together. Range gates target selection, launch, and impact. A dead or out-of-range focus target advances deterministically only to an eligible in-range target; area skills keep their defined multiplicity inside the attack line.
- Seconds-based buffs, debuffs, DoT/HoT, barriers and interruption expire and tick at reproducible times, including across waves. Surviving party HP does not refill on a wave clear; a party wipe resets HP and the stage wave as specified.
- A saved hero loads with level, EXP, HP and gear intact; the five selected active skills are available without individual unlocks. Legacy SP/FLOW keys are ignored and not written back.
- Kill-level fixtures credit Gold and EXP exactly once for basics, skills, counters and DoT, including defeat/retry, reload, overlapping waves and boss transitions. Boss progression grants no unapproved milestone resource reward.
- In-app browser QA covers fresh combat, two waves, boss availability/challenge, death/retry, pause/resume and five active skills per hero. Focused unit/integration tests, hot-file mirror gate, WASM shadow ABI check and final-state browser error check pass. Leave changes uncommitted until human QA approval.

## Approved tower-defense kits (owner approval: 2026-09-24)

These replace the earlier JRPG-shaped kit draft. Each hero is a stationary attack source with a separate basic shot and five auto-firing active skills, available from the start. Fara absorbs enemy pressure, Hondo exploits vulnerable targets and overlapping shots like a scrappy thief, Runa controls areas, and Kaja modifies allied towers. The existing arena has enemies approach and stop to fight. These kits add no lane, slot, deployment-tile, path-leak, or wind-up rule.

Area attacks use the existing 360 × 640 combat reference space and scale with the arena. At launch, lock a target point or piercing line. At impact, include each living enemy whose center lies inside the stated circle, rectangle, or line width; resolve each once. A blast still lands at its locked point if its original target dies. Single-target basics retain their separately approved impact rules. Skills without a valid and useful target remain ready and spend no cooldown. Allied target ties use stable encounter order. The seconds, widths, radii, damage values and triggers below are approved for implementation. Preserve internal save keys.

## Approved displacement behavior

Shield Slam pushes, Frost Reversal forces a short retreat, and Return Gate teleports. All three move enemy world X only and retain the enemy's current Y. Moving away or walking back prevents new enemy attacks; cooldowns still follow the frozen-aware simulation clock, and a ready attack can start on return to the stopping point. A launched projectile continues. Enemies at the right-side boundary receive damaging hits where applicable, while Return Gate remains ready until it can move a target at least 40 units.
