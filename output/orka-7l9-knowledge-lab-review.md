**The lab's top-level `NOT PROVEN` verdict is justified.** It establishes useful fixture outcomes and exposes a serious cap/HP interaction. It does not certify early progression or satisfying combat.

| Finding | What the evidence supports | Limit for design judgment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter no-Kaja farming | Five seeds completed 20 ordinary clears with zero Hero KOs, gaps or deadlocks. | All 100 clears repeated Stage 1-1; every positive applied hit was one damage. This is a bounded survival regression, not proof of pressure or survival across early stages. |
| Defensive gear sensitivity | In matched-seed, Auto-Equip-off gear fixtures, the base had 5 Hero KOs, `+10 DEF` had 1 and `+30 HP` had 11. With `+30 HP`, observed Falie/Huun hits included many two-damage events where the base had one. [`routineEnemyScaling.mjs`](../web-runner/src/core/routineEnemyScaling.mjs) raises the routine per-hit ceiling from one to two at max HP 100. | The ceiling step is a real rule and makes added HP potentially adverse. KO totals are not a clean causal estimate: combat timing/targeting can diverge despite matching seeds, and several gear sessions stalled. The exact fraction of hits bound by the cap remains unknown because pre-cap damage is not observed. |
| Kaja | Level-1 runs had zero source-attributed skill healing, as expected from the level-15 Patchwork unlock. The new level-15 runs also had zero because the trigger was not reached. | This does not overturn the earlier wounded level-15 proof of effective healing in the [gauntlet ledger](orka-7l9-gauntlet-progress.md). A wounded, matched party is needed to assess benefit. |
| First boss | The predeclared C5 test passes: synthetic three-Hero, fixed-gear level 1 timed out in five seeds, levels 2–5 cleared, and earned four-Hero Auto-Equip arrivals cleared in five seeds. | The synthetic and earned parties differ in size and gear. The earned level-1 party won on its first attempt every time, so the lab has not demonstrated an earned failure, upgrade, then win sequence. C5's fixture `PASS` must not be read as solved player-facing boss pacing. |
| Recovery | The initial KO triggered a full-party restore on the same stage/wave in all five seeds. Four seeds then suffered another Hero KO after 2–11 later ordinary clears; the fifth stopped making combat/clear progress. | This fails the lab's stronger five-seed, 20-clear post-recovery gate. It does not show a restore-placement defect. Repeated losses may be encounter pressure; the long stall needs separate reproduction before assigning a game-side cause. |
| Silent sessions | Four of 70 prospective sessions had 3,166–7,089 simulated seconds without a combat event or clear while the harness's progress-signature deadlock detector stayed quiet. | This is a candidate stall, not a proven production softlock or root cause. Reproduce at normal speed, inspect actor/action state and scheduler clocks, and separate harness artifacts from game behavior. |

The shadow model accounts for logged HP changes under its declared assumptions. That validates accounting and provenance, not attack cadence, player feel, StoneAge parity, or balance. The durable sample includes compressed observed/model streams; its manifest also points to raw reports under `/private/tmp/`, which should be pinned or regenerated before final review.

## Next proof order, if implementation resumes

1. Reproduce and classify the silent-session failure on the exact candidate SHA, including a normal-speed run. Make the deadlock detector sensitive to no combat events or clears while ignoring legitimate boss transitions. Keep any gameplay fix in a separately reviewed step.
2. Measure the HP-100 cap discontinuity with fixed gear and repeated matched openings. Report per-Hero max HP, one- versus two-damage hit counts, KOs, target changes and stalls. Compare HP, DEF and offense variants without treating a matched seed as an identical action trace. Retain `raw=?` and `cap=?` until observed.
3. Trace an earned opening through first-boss arrival with party size and gear accumulation exposed. Decide the intended first-boss pressure from the owner contract, then test that path; the three-Hero synthetic sweep remains a separate control.
4. Recheck post-recovery endurance after the stall is understood, with representative stage/gear/level fixtures and the same-stage rule. Obtain a wounded level-15 Kaja observation separately.
5. Keep the design verdict open until a pressure criterion is approved and the recovery/progression gates pass. The present five fixed seeds are regression evidence, not a population confidence bound.
