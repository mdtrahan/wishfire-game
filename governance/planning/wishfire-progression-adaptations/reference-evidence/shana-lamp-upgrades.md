# Verified Shana upgrade evidence

Observed on 2026-09-30 through the player-facing in-app browser. Screenshots were captured; there is no continuous video for this follow-up. Purchased coins funded part of the test, so it does not measure free-play affordability.

| Rarity | Tier 1 | Tier 2 confirmed | Tier 3 preview |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Normal |80%|73.5%|68%|
| High Normal |15%|17%|20%|
| Rare |5%|8%|10%|
| HR |0%|1.5%|2%|
| SR and above |0%|0%|0%|

Tier 1→2 required two 3,000-coin purchases, totaling 6,000. Level Up started a five-minute countdown. The tutorial hand directed Speed Up All. One five-minute ticket was consumed and completed the upgrade immediately. Surplus ticket time treatment remains unverified. Tier 2→3 shows 7,000 coins per progress purchase; its total cost, purchase count and timer remain unverified.

Hero Lv5, EXP319/700 and CP20,743 stayed unchanged across the device upgrade. Its gameplay badge became 2. The panel displayed rarity benefits, with no storage or direct stat bonus shown. Benefits elsewhere remain uninvestigated.

Coins accumulated during combat. Replaced Normal gear appeared to supply coins, with combat confounding exact yield; 96 EXP was visible. The authorized test exchanged 350 existing diamonds for 10,000 coins. Five-minute tickets sell in quantities 5/20/100/500 for 150/600/3,000/15,000 diamonds. No real-money payment completed.
