# 03 — Skill acquisition research hold

Status: incomplete research; no feature goal ready for approval.

Only one skill was unlocked/exercised during the study. The five-slot unlock sequence, binding conditions, skill gacha and ally gacha were not traced. Proposed milestones 2/5/8/12/16 and a free per-hero Unlock action are withdrawn from the adaptation plan. Existing Wishfire kits remain intact.

## Evidence needed before new stories

Operate the reference until all five skill slots are available. At every transition record player level, stage, task completion, displayed condition and the action that opens the slot. Distinguish slot availability, skill ownership, equipping and upgrading. Where conditions overlap, report uncertainty rather than infer a formula.

Use the skill and ally acquisition screens. Record currencies, cost per draw, guarantees if shown, duplicate handling, item descriptions before/after acquisition and conditions for placing skills/allies into slots. Explain which system controls each progression step. Record the relevant gameplay with timestamps.

After this evidence, propose the smallest Wishfire adaptation, identify its differences from the reference and write fresh user stories for owner review. No implementation is authorized by this research checklist.
