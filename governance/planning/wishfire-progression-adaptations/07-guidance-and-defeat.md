# 07 — Guidance through the lamp loop

**Goal:** Extend the existing helper through equipment review, dismantling and lamp upgrade preview.

## Proposed behavior

Guide lamp draw → storage → hero comparison → equipment → dismantling → lamp upgrade preview. Complete steps from their real state changes, once. An upgrade preview does not force a resource spend. When an upgrade becomes affordable, point to its exact benefits and cost, then acknowledge the resulting tier change. The reference teaches a speedup ticket; this proposal includes no timer or ticket tutorial.

Preserve the blue helper hand, bob and focus rings. After 15 seconds idle it points to Hondo during initial hero selection, then Auto Equip after a hero has been selected. Any input dismisses it. If its target is unavailable, choose a useful enabled control from current state.

## Acceptance

Record the idle threshold, dismissal and retargeting. Reload must not repeat tutorial rewards. Show guidance without replacing the equip cache or interrupting the reward banner.

## Dependencies and approval

Depends on equipment and lamp upgrade eligibility. Skill prompts await the skill research hold. Defeat advice is withdrawn because it was not observed. Paid offers and premium currencies remain outside this proposal.
